About

Aspiring biomedical engineer focused on healthcare technology

I am a final-year B.E. Biomedical Engineering student at Agni College of Technology, Chennai. My academic interests centre on biomedical instrumentation, medical devices, hospital equipment, and the growing role of Python and biomedical IoT in healthcare.

Through hospital exposure at Dr. Mehta's Hospital, I have observed CT scan and X-ray imaging workflows, ICU equipment environments (including HFNC), and equipment safety practices within biomedical engineering operations. Alongside this, I have worked on academic and hackathon projects spanning IoT-based safety systems, smart toll identification, and thermal imaging for healthcare research.

I am currently developing my technical foundation and looking for opportunities to learn from practising biomedical engineers and contribute to healthcare technology projects.
